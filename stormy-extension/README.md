# Stormy Walk for the browser

Stormy, the orange-and-white tabby, keeps watch while your AI chat works in the browser: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Copilot, Mistral and Grok.

- She types on a little laptop while the AI writes a reply, and says "Done!" with a soft meow when it's finished.
- If you're on another tab, a notification with Stormy and her envelope tells you the reply is ready.
- Tiny cats walk across the chat. Click "✕ cats" to hide them, or the 🐾 in the corner to bring everything back.
- Drag Stormy anywhere (she remembers the spot on each site), point at her and tap the ✕ to hide her.
- The toolbar icon has switches for Stormy, the cats and the meows.

It only reads the page to spot the AI's "stop" button. It sends nothing anywhere.

## Install (Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave)

1. Download this folder (on GitHub: Code → Download ZIP, then unzip it).
2. Open `chrome://extensions` (in Opera: `opera://extensions`).
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the `stormy-extension` folder.
5. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and say hi to Stormy.

The meows are cut from "Meow of a pleading cat" on Wikimedia Commons, released into the public domain by its author.
